title:  "Hotel Eggs"
image: hotel-eggs.jpg

tags:
- breakfast

ingredients:
- Pasture-raised eggs
- Butter

directions:
- Preheat a non-stick or stainless steel pan over medium heat
- Add butter and coat entire pan
- While pan is heating up, crack and whisk the eggs with a fork. The goal is to whip some air into them.
- When the pan is sizzling, turn the heat down to low and quickly add the eggs.
- Stir the eggs around until cooked. They will cook quickly on the pre-heated pan.
- Remove from heat when the eggs are still a little wet or glossy. Don't overcook.
- Season with salt and pepper or your seasoning of choice.
- Enjoy by themselves or over some good sourdough toast.
